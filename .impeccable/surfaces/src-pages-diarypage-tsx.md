Scope: Diary screen, Operate mode. Layout changes on the Diary only; colors and fonts apply app-wide (owner's choice). Owner: one person, phone and desktop, several quick logs a day. Task: see kcal left and log the next meal at once.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The day stacks up like produce: each meal owns a color, and the budget bar shows where the calories went. Refuses the category's ring-plus-cards arrangement and the old gauge cluster.

OWN-WORLD: Forest green (light) or aubergine (dark) field; squash, tomato, plum and leaf meal colors; chunky Bricolage Grotesque numerals and meal names; Barlow for food names; open hairline rows on a pale celery or deep aubergine ground; round filled add buttons.

STORY: The owner opens the app, reads kcal left, sees which meals used the budget, taps once to log the next meal, and leaves.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed field holds date nav, kcal left at 76px, eaten and budget at right, the 100-kcal-cell budget bar, three macro bars and an eating-window row. Open meal lists follow. A docked Log button in the next meal's color sits above the tab bar; desktop puts the field in a sticky left column.
